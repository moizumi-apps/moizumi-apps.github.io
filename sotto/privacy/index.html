Sotto Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Publisher: moizumi apps
Contact: moizumiapps@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

This app does not collect any information that can identify you, such as your name, address, phone number, email address, or location. There is no account registration.

2. Saved photo and settings

You choose the photo to place behind the mist from the photos on your iPhone. The camera is used only if you choose to take a photo. The photo you choose and your settings (sound, sky color, lock, and sound on/off) are saved only on your iPhone. They are not sent to any external server. If you delete the app, they are deleted too.

3. Sharing, advertising, and analytics

We do not share any information with third parties. The app has no advertising and no usage analytics. The app does not connect to the internet.

4. Changes

If anything changes, such as adding purchases or advertising, we will update this page.
